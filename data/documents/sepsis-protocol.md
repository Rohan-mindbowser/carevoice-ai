# Hospital Sepsis Protocol

## Recognition
Screen patients for sepsis when infection is suspected alongside two or more SIRS criteria:
temperature above 38°C or below 36°C, heart rate over 90 beats per minute, respiratory rate over
20 breaths per minute, or abnormal white blood cell count. Escalate immediately if the patient shows
signs of organ dysfunction such as hypotension, altered mental status, or elevated lactate.

## One-Hour Sepsis Bundle
Initiate the sepsis bundle within one hour of recognition. Measure serum lactate. Obtain blood
cultures before administering antibiotics. Administer broad-spectrum intravenous antibiotics.
Begin rapid administration of 30 mL per kilogram of crystalloid fluid for hypotension or a lactate
of 4 mmol/L or higher. Reassess volume status and tissue perfusion after the fluid bolus.

## Escalation
If hypotension persists after adequate fluid resuscitation, start vasopressors to maintain a mean
arterial pressure of at least 65 mmHg. Norepinephrine is the first-line vasopressor. Notify the
rapid response team and consider transfer to the intensive care unit.

## Reassessment
Re-measure lactate within two to four hours if the initial value was elevated. Document the source
of infection and de-escalate antibiotics once culture results are available.
